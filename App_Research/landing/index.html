The project

Music that moves with the world around you.

Music can shape the way we experience exercise, but a fixed playlist cannot respond to changing surroundings or movement. In Tune explores how people, places, and music can work together by first understanding which environments different pieces of music best suit.

It uses GPS to recognise the runner's surroundings and a wearable device to monitor running rhythm and ambient sound. By bringing these together, the system matches music to the environment, selects music around the runner's pace, and balances playback volume with the surrounding sound.

A responsive loop

Place, pace, and sound become one soundtrack.

01

Sense place

GPS and OpenStreetMap recognise forest and mountain, water, park, urban, or road environments.

02

Read movement

A wearable device sends running rhythm and surrounding sound to the app over Bluetooth.

03

Match music

Environment tags and a flexible BPM range select music that fits both the place and the runner.

04

Adapt playback

Music streams to the phone while output volume responds to the ambient sound level.

Behind the experience

Built as a connected system.

In Tune combines a Flutter Android application, GPS, Overpass API context detection, a custom BLE sensing device, preprocessed music metadata, and online audio streaming.
